## [Unreleased]

## [0.9.0] - 2026-10-31

### Changed
- **Onboarding in two steps** (docs/ONBOARDING.md): after «Tu cuenta está lista» («2 pasos más:») come «Persona cuidada» (Paso 1 de 2, `POST /api/hogar`) and «Consentimiento» (Paso 2 de 2); then «Todo listo» shows the consent certificate (CA-05.1, CA-05.3), asks for the notifications after explaining them (CA-16.2) and lists what is left: «Sin PC vinculada», «Sin contacto secundario», or «Falta el consentimiento» after «Ahora no». Linking the PC, inviting a family member and the notifications are no longer setup steps; they stay on Inicio («Agregar PC», «Activar notificaciones»), Ajustes › «Cámaras y PCs» and Familia. The consent form, its two boxes and its record are unchanged, and the movements consent (text version 3) stays separate in Ajustes › Privacidad.
- The pairing screens (link a PC, scan, type the code, approve, choose the cameras, room name) lost their setup mode (`config=1`, «Paso 3 de 5»); the routes `/configuracion/constancia`, `/configuracion/camara`, `/configuracion/familia` and `/configuracion/avisos` are gone.

### Added
- **Welcome photo:** the prototype's photo of a daughter embracing her mother above «Cuida a tu familiar aunque no estés en casa.», 4:3 with rounded corners, labelled «Una hija abraza a su madre» for screen readers (`assets/imagenes/bienvenida.webp`, 640 × 480, 29 KB; Pexels photo by Danik Prihodko under the Pexels license, credited in `assets/imagenes/CREDITOS.md`).

## [0.8.0] - 2026-10-31

### Changed
- **Consent texts name Microsoft Azure as the cloud store:** the movements consent is text version `3` («Dónde»: «… en la nube: Microsoft Azure (Azure Blob Storage, región Chile Central), con servidores fuera del Perú. …»), sent as `versionTexto` and matching the API's `versionTextoVigente` `3`; a consent given with version `2` (Cloudflare R2) or `1` keeps showing its own text. The monitoring consent («Qué guardamos») and the privacy policy («Dónde se guardan») name Azure instead of Cloudflare R2; everything else is unchanged
- **One color for every icon:** rows, tiles, chevrons, toolbars, the tab bar, help «?», dialogs and notices use a single token (`Paleta.icono`, the brand violet). Severity, connectivity and success icons keep their state color, and icons over video or the alert flood keep their own foreground. The selected tab is no longer coral: it shows with its pill and its label.
- **Banded cards are filled with the color of their band:** the alert card, Inicio's status card and the consent records are red, green, amber, brown, slate or violet all over, not only their top band. What is inside reads on the fill (white on all but amber, which takes ink, all at 4.5:1 or better); severity tags and buttons invert on it. The striped (paused) and dashed (disconnected) top bands are now solid: the state still shows with its icon and text.

### Added
- **Swipe a floating notice away:** toasts and in-app notifications close with a sideways swipe (either way) or a swipe up, besides their timeout; a tap still runs the notice's action. Screen readers get the system dismiss action and «Cerrar aviso» (new copy, docs/BLOCKERS.md). The swipe cancels the timeout and only closes the notice it started on, never one that replaced it. Hiding an alert's notice («Rosa sigue en el suelo», escalations) marks nothing: the alert stays active on its screen and on Inicio.

### Fixed
- The weekly branch cleanup listed each branch's pull requests with `gh api -f`, which sends a POST to the create-pull-request endpoint, so every lookup failed with 422 and the open-PR and `do-not-delete` protections were never checked. It now lists with `--method GET`, and a branch whose PRs cannot be read is left alone.

Only one registration runs now; calls made meanwhile wait for a single one that runs right after it and get its result. A token the phone no longer uses is deleted from the backend (`DELETE /api/dispositivos/{tokenPush}`).
- **Registration on every start and return.** `POST /api/dispositivos` is sent on every start and every return to the foreground, so the backend records when the phone was last seen (`vistoEn`) and a token FCM changed silently (on the web `onTokenRefresh` never fires) is registered. - **Errors were swallowed.** A Firebase error while getting the token (other than iOS still waiting for APNs) or a failed registration is no longer ignored: Inicio and Notificaciones say «Este celular no recibe las alertas» with «Activar notificaciones», which registers again.
- **«Notificaciones activadas» from the permission alone.** Notificaciones and the confirmation toast said the alerts would arrive with the app closed once the browser or system allowed notifications, even when the backend had no active device. New copy in `docs/BLOCKERS.md`.
- **A lost push left the alert unseen with the app open.** Inicio asks for the active alert every 20 s while it is visible and the app is in the foreground, so the tab shell opens a new alert whose push never arrived.
- **«Seguimos reintentando» when nothing retried.** The alert screen and its timeline said the notification was being sent again whenever `notificadaEn` was empty. They now follow `estadoAviso` (contract §5): «Seguimos reintentando el envío» only with `REINTENTANDO`; with `NO_ENTREGADO` the alert says it did not arrive as a notification and the timeline «La notificación no se pudo entregar»; nothing while it is `ENVIANDO`.
- **Android channel and iOS foreground.** `MainActivity` creates the notification channel `alertas_caida` («Alertas», high importance, public on the lock screen) that the backend sends alert pushes to, and the manifest makes it the default FCM channel, so a fall is a heads-up notification with sound. On iOS a push that arrives with the app open also shows the system banner and plays its sound (`setForegroundNotificationPresentationOptions`).
- **PWA notifications while a window is visible.** The Firebase SDK shows nothing when a window of the app is visible, so a fall arriving while the PWA was open in the background or another tab never reached the screen. The service worker now shows every push itself, with the backend's `tag` (the alert's id) so a later notice of the same alert replaces the earlier one and Firebase's own copy replaces it without alerting twice; urgent notices stay until dismissed (`requireInteraction`). ### Changed

### Fixed
- Live view: a playback error or a stream that stops advancing for 10 s, or whose first frame takes over 20 s, is no longer ignored: the session ends and the disconnected view offers «Ver en vivo», which opens a new one. Opening retries while the camera starts publishing.
- Live view: the whole 4:3 frame is shown at its own aspect ratio instead of being cropped to 3:4.
- Live view: the session is ended with `DELETE` when the app goes to the background, and a new one opens on return.
- Live view: `409 SIN_CONSENTIMIENTO` shows «La cámara está detenida» and reloads the household (CA-05.2).
- Revocation: `404 SIN_CONSENTIMIENTO` reloads Privacidad, which shows «Sin consentimiento», instead of an error toast; the card title no longer overflows on narrow screens.
